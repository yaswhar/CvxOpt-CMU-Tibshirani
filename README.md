## About This Repository

My solutions to homework assignments for **Convex Optimization: Fall 2019** at Carnegie Mellon University. The course covers the fundamentals of convex optimization, with topics spanning convex sets and functions, duality theory, first‑order methods, second‑order methods, and advanced topics such as ADMM, coordinate descent, and stochastic gradient descent.

Each homework folder contains my solution PDF (with typeset mathematical proofs) and any accompanying code. The official course materials are available on the [course website](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~ryantibs/convexopt/).

## Course Topics

- **Theory I – Fundamentals:** Convex sets and functions, optimization basics, canonical problem forms (LPs, QPs, SOCPs, SDPs)
- **Algorithms I – First‑Order Methods:** Gradient descent, subgradient method, proximal gradient descent, stochastic gradient descent
- **Theory II – Duality & Optimality:** Duality in LPs and general programs, KKT conditions, dual correspondences
- **Algorithms II – Second‑Order Methods:** Newton's method, barrier method, primal‑dual interior‑point methods, quasi‑Newton methods
- **Advanced Topics:** Numerical linear algebra, coordinate descent, dual decomposition, ADMM, Frank‑Wolfe, modern stochastic methods
